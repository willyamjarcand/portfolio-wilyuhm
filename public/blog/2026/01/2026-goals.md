# 2026 Goals

New year, new list of things I'll probably half-finish.

## Ship more, polish less

I have a bad habit of getting 80% done on something and losing steam before it's actually out. This year I want to bias toward "done and shipped" over "perfect and unreleased."

## Write more

Starting this blog is part of that. Even short posts count. The goal is one post a month — not a high bar, but consistent.

## Go deeper on systems

I've spent most of my career in product-land (Rails, React, CRUD apps). I want to get more comfortable lower in the stack — compilers, networking, OS internals. Not to switch careers, just to be a better engineer.

## Stay curious

That's the one I always keep.
