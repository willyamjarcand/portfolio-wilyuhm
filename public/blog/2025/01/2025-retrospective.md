# 2025 Retrospective

Looking back at the year before writing down what I want from the next one.

## What went well

**Shipped the portfolio.** It took longer than expected but it's out and people seem to like it. The Win95 aesthetic was a risk — it could have read as gimmicky — but the feedback has been that it's memorable in a good way.

**Got comfortable with TypeScript.** I used to treat it as "JavaScript with annotations." Now I lean on the type system to design before I code. Different mindset entirely.

**Read more.** Finished *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, *A Philosophy of Software Design*, and half of *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs*. Will finish SICP in 2026.

## What didn't

**Too much context-switching.** I spread myself across too many projects and didn't go deep enough on any of them. The work I'm most proud of came from sustained focus, not variety.

**Didn't write.** I kept meaning to start a blog and didn't. That changes in 2026.

## The number that matters

Hours of flow state per week. Everything else is downstream of that.
